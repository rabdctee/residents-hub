MRC Refurbishment and a Friendly Reminder

Our MRC refurbishment is well underway, and we've now made it through the first week, which was expected to be the noisiest! There's still plenty happening behind the scenes, and we appreciate everyone's patience and understanding as the work continues. We're looking forward to seeing the finished result and sharing the refreshed space with you all.

Importantly, I'd like to take this opportunity to remind everyone that a little kindness goes a long way. We understand that sometimes there can be frustration and that we won't always agree on everything. However, how we communicate with one another matters. Our Charter outlines the expectations we have around treating staff, fellow residents and visitors with courtesy, dignity, and respect. Our Charter is located in the foyer of the Manor.

Everyone deserves to feel comfortable, respected, and safe within our community, including our staff, who are here each day doing their best to support you. We don't have to agree on everything, but we do need to treat each other respectfully.

To help maintain the appearance and enjoyment of our village for all residents, we'd like to remind everyone to be mindful of the Village Rules outlined in your Welcome Booklet.

Recently, we have noticed an increase in items that do not align with village guidelines, including:

  • Clotheslines and washing visible from common areas
  • Signs and notices displayed without approval
  • Unauthorised changes to gardens and landscaping
  • Storage of personal items in outdoor or common areas

These guidelines are in place to ensure our community remains neat, attractive and enjoyable for everyone. Please consider this as a general reminder. If ongoing concerns are identified, residents may receive a reminder notice in their letterbox, followed by a visit from Village Management to discuss the matter if required.

Thank you to everyone who continues to show patience, kindness, and consideration. It really does make a difference to our community.

Warm regards, Candice.

Council Cabs

To book a Council Cab, phone 07 3403 2227.

About Council Cabs

Council offers a shared transport service for those who find it difficult to access essentials, such as grocery shopping, banking or post office services.

Council Cabs transports eligible users to and from shopping centres within their local area on set days and times.

Each one-way trip costs between $1 to $2.50, to be paid to the driver before departure.

Council Cabs eligibility

You can use Council Cabs if you:

  • are 60 years or older, or
  • have a mobility impairment, or
  • hold a Pensioner Concession Card or Commonwealth Seniors Health Card.

Important things to know

  • Mobility aid and wheelchair accessible vehicles are available on request.
  • A child under the care of an eligible passenger can travel for free.
  • Council Cabs honour the Queensland Government Companion Card, allowing free travel for carers.
  • Each passenger is limited to 3 green/reusable or 5 plastic shopping bags (or equivalent) per trip. This is to ensure that there is enough space for all users.
  • Council Cabs does not operate on public holidays.
  • The cut off for reservations is 12 noon on the day before travel.
  • Advise if you have mobility equipment or require a wheelchair accessible taxi.
  • Advise if you are travelling with a companion or carer.
  • You can inform Council Cabs if you would like your reservation to be permanent.

Council Cabs schedule

Check the online schedule to find available Council Cabs services in your area. You can search online using your destination or suburb — Council Cabs | Brisbane City Council.

Community Text Message Service

The Residents Committee is considering introducing a simple text message service to help important information reach residents quickly and reliably when time matters.

The text message service is intended for practical village information where a prompt message may be helpful. It is not intended for general discussion, advertising, or frequent routine notices. The service would be used only for important or time-sensitive matters, such as urgent safety information, interruption to essential services — such as loss of power or water — or changes to scheduled meetings or social events.

The service would support our existing noticeboards, Residents' Hub and emails — it would not replace them.

Key points

  • Messages will be brief and only sent from this number: 0468 104 543. If you receive a text message from this number, you can be sure it is authentic. You may wish to save it in your phone contacts with a name like 'BDRV Text Service' or similar.
  • At this stage, messages would only be sent by the Chair of the Residents Committee, the Chair of the Body Corporate, or the Convenor of the Social Club Committee.
  • The service is scheduled to commence on the 1st October, 2026.

Participation

  • Participation is optional. If you do not wish to receive these messages, you may opt out at any time.
  • Unless you choose to opt out, messages would be sent to the mobile number currently listed for you on the Residents Contact List. If you would prefer us to use a different mobile number, please let the Residents Committee know.

How to Opt Out

You may opt out now or at any later time. If you have any questions about the proposed service, please speak with any member of the Residents Committee. You can opt out in either of the following ways:

  • Complete a slip and place it in the Residents Committee mailbox at the Manor.
  • Email your name and unit number to the Residents Committee at rabd.ctee@gmail.com.

Rick Van Haeften, Chair — Residents Committee, Bridgeman Downs Retirement Village

Raise a Concern or Suggestion

The Residents Committee has introduced a service for residents to raise a concern, issue, or suggestion with the Committee.

The intent is simply to enable residents to feel comfortable with raising matters of concern, or to make suggestions, knowing that they will be kept private and taken seriously.

Key points

  • All items go privately to the Chair of the Residents Committee — they are not shared publicly. The Chair may respond to you directly and/or bring the matter to the next Residents Committee meeting for discussion.
  • Before submitting an item, you'll be asked to take a quick look at the 'What We're Working On' list, in case your concern is already known about and being worked on.
  • Look for 'Raise a Concern' in the sidebar menu on the Residents Information Hub — you'll need your usual Residents PIN to open it.

This service works best through the Residents Information Hub, as there are links to other areas on the Hub. The Hub can be accessed by typing bdrv.au into your web browser (Chrome/Safari/Microsoft Edge or other).

However, should you wish to submit an item in writing, please address it to: Chair of the Residents Committee and pop it in the Residents Committee mailbox at the Manor. The mailbox is clearly labelled.

If you have any trouble using the service, please don't hesitate to ask.

Rick Van Haeften, Chair — Residents Committee, Bridgeman Downs Retirement Village

The Month That Was

Spring was welcomed with almost 80 residents attending a morning tea and Fashion Parade presented by Tulio Fashions on 2 September. I hope everyone is enjoying wearing their new purchases. Approximately 50 residents then spent an enjoyable afternoon in Fountain Court on 14 September at a Spring Garden Party: tasty food, enjoyable music and pleasant company. On 24 September the Sip and Paint afternoon saw residents sipping on various beverages, nibbling from food platters while exhibiting their artistic talent painting a variety of different ceramic planters. The weekly Happy Hours continued to be well attended while a sausage sizzle during the AFL Grand Final concluded the month's events. Photos taken at these events have been posted to the Residents' Information Hub (bdrv.au/event-gallery.html) where any resident is welcome to post photos taken at Aveo Bridgeman Downs events and activities.

Of course, another significant aspect of September was the closing of the Melody Ridge Centre for refurbishment and the relocation of Social Club property. Thanks to all residents that assisted with the packing up and movement of items.
