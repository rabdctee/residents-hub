Why Chromebooks?

  • Residents sign in as a guest — nothing personal is stored after sign-out
  • No viruses, no software installation, no IT maintenance required
  • Starts up in seconds; very simple to use
  • Long battery life (10–11 hours); lightweight and durable

Considerations

  • Location & furniture: A suitable table and chairs are needed
  • Internet access: Aveo's agreement will be required for reliable WiFi
  • Printing: Optional shared printer (~$80–$150 additional)
  • Supervision: Largely self-service; a printed guide for residents can be provided
