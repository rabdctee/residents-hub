Village Bus Replacement — Your Views

As mentioned by Justine Hughes in her last two visits, options are being explored to replace the current village bus with a modern, comfortable 12-seater minibus. The current bus requires a special bus driver's licence, which means Gary is our only driver — limiting how often outings can happen. A smaller bus can be driven by any staff member or resident with a standard driver's licence, meaning more drivers, more flexibility, and more outings. Other positives include easier access to shopping centres, all seats in use, less parking space needed, and a newer vehicle that costs less to maintain. The main concerns that have been raised are capacity — 12 seats versus the current bus and storage of walkers. Both issues are being considered and your Community Manager and Residents Committee are working through options that may include, a booking system and running two trips on popular days. We'd like to know your overall view before any decisions are made.
