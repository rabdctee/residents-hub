📋 Before you upload

Documents are uploaded using a secure Google Form. Follow the steps below, then click the button to open the form. When you are done, close the form tab to return here.

1
Save your document as a PDF if it is not already in that format.
2
Name the file clearly — see the naming guide below.
3
Sign in with your committee Google account before opening the form. For security, the form will not accept uploads from personal accounts.
4
Click the button below to open the upload form in a new tab. When finished, close that tab to return to this page.
📤 Open Upload Form
💡 You will receive a confirmation email once your upload is complete. If you do not receive one within a few minutes, check your committee email inbox. Contact Rick if you experience any issues.
