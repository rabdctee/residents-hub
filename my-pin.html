🔑 My PIN

Your personal PIN lets you into the parts of the Hub you've been given access to. It's private — no one else can see it, not even the Hub administrator.

Use this the first time, or if you've forgotten your PIN. Ask Rick, the Hub administrator, for a one-off welcome code.

If you know your current PIN, you can change it here at any time.

Tips for a good PIN

  • Avoid easy ones like 1234, 0000 or your unit number.
  • Don't use the same PIN as your bank card.
  • Forgotten it? No problem — ask Rick for a new welcome code and set a fresh PIN above.
