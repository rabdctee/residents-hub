July – September 2026  ·  Prepared for residents by the Residents' Committee

This summary covers the first quarter of the 2026–27 financial year (1 July – 30 September 2026). Aveo provides five separate financial reports for Bridgeman Downs — one for each area of the village. We've translated those reports into plain language so you can see where money comes from and where it goes in your area.

All figures are year-to-date (the full quarter) unless otherwise noted. The closing balance is the cash held in the fund at 30 September 2026.
