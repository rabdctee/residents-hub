🎉 Social Life
What is the Social Club?
The Social Club is run by a small group of enthusiastic resident volunteers who organise events and activities to keep life in our community fun, friendly and connected. From morning teas and movie afternoons to happy hours and other Social Club events — there is always something to look forward to. Everyone is welcome, and new ideas are always appreciated!

📋 Residents Committee
What is the Residents Committee?
The Residents Committee (RC) is a group of volunteers elected by residents to represent your interests and work with village management on issues that matter to you. The committee meets regularly and keeps all residents informed.
