Rugby Union — Super Rugby & Wallabies

Wallabies beat the Springboks! · Next: Bledisloe Cup, 10 & 17 October
TEST SEASON ON ▼

The Super Rugby Pacific season finished back in June. The Wallabies are now well into a big 14-Test year — after home Tests against Ireland, France and Italy in July and a series in Japan in August, they won the Puma Trophy in Argentina (a win and a draw — their first-ever series win there), then beat world champions South Africa 42–38 in a thriller in Perth on 27 September, despite being down to 11 men late in the game. Next is the Bledisloe Cup against New Zealand — Auckland on 10 October, then Sydney on 17 October. Super Rugby and Wallabies Tests are broadcast live on Stan Sport (subscription).

Cricket

Home summer starting · Women's ODIs in Brisbane 9–14 Oct · Shield from 6 Oct
SUMMER STARTING ▼
🏏 Australian women play Bangladesh — right here in Brisbane
The Australian women's team host Bangladesh in three One Day Internationals at Allan Border Field, Albion — Friday 9, Sunday 11 and Wednesday 14 October, all starting 1:50pm. Three T20 matches follow in Sydney (18, 20 & 22 October). Back in August, the men's winter series against Bangladesh finished level at 1–1: Bangladesh won a shock first Test in Darwin, then Australia hit back to win in Mackay inside two days, led by Mitchell Starc's 10 wickets.
🏆 Congratulations to the Australian Women!
Australia won the ICC Women's T20 World Cup 2026, beating England by 7 wickets in the final at Lord's on 5 July. Beth Mooney was Player of the Match and Player of the Series.

The Australian cricket summer is getting started. The state One-Day Cup began on 18 September, the Sheffield Shield starts on 6 October, the women's WBBL begins 29 October and the men's Big Bash League starts 12 December. The main men's international summer follows later in the year. Cricket Australia's website has fixtures and results year-round; live coverage of most cricket in Australia is on Fox Cricket / Kayo (subscription), with the Big Bash League also free on Channel 7 / 7plus.
