FIFA World Cup 2026

11 June – 19 July 2026 · USA, Canada & Mexico · Round of 16 begins Sunday 5 July
ROUND OF 16 ▼
🇦🇺 The Socceroos' World Cup campaign has ended
  • Sun 14 June — Australia 2–0 Türkiye · Vancouver
  • Sat 20 June — Australia 0–2 USA · Seattle
  • Fri 26 June — Australia 0–0 Paraguay · San Francisco
  • Sat 4 July — Australia 1–1 Egypt (Egypt won 4–2 on penalties) · Dallas
A gutsy campaign for Tony Popovic's young side — they made the knockout rounds for only the third time ever, and were desperately unlucky to go out on penalties in their first-ever World Cup shootout. Well done, Socceroos.

The Round of 32 is complete and the tournament now moves into the Round of 16 — 16 teams left, one loss and you're on the plane home. Great news for Australian fans: SBS has exclusive rights and every single match, right through to the Final, is completely free to watch, both on TV and online.

US OPEN THIS WEEK ▼
