Cricket

Australia v Bangladesh Tests on now · Full home summer from October
TEST MATCH ON ▼
🏏 A rare winter Test series — Australia v Bangladesh
Bangladesh are touring Australia for the first time in 23 years, playing two Tests in Australia's north — the first of their kind since 2004. Bangladesh won the 1st Test in Darwin by 9 wickets, a big upset. The 2nd Test is being played right now in Mackay (22–26 August). Live on Fox Cricket / Kayo.
🏆 Congratulations to the Australian Women!
Australia won the ICC Women's T20 World Cup 2026, beating England by 7 wickets in the final at Lord's on 5 July. Beth Mooney was Player of the Match and Player of the Series.

Outside this one-off winter tour, Australian domestic and international cricket is still largely between seasons — the full home summer (Sheffield Shield, Big Bash League and the main international Test season) begins in October. Cricket Australia's website has fixtures and results year-round; live coverage of most cricket in Australia is on Fox Cricket / Kayo (subscription), with the Big Bash League also free on Channel 7 / 7plus.

Athletics

Commonwealth Games complete · Diamond League season continues
GAMES COMPLETE ▼
🏆 Commonwealth Games Athletics — wrapped up
Track and field at Glasgow 2026 finished on 1 August at Scotstoun Stadium, with Australia topping the athletics medal table. Rose Davies became the first woman in Commonwealth Games history to win both the 5000m and 10,000m.

Outside of major Games, Australian athletes also compete on the international Diamond League circuit through the (northern) summer, and domestically in the Australian Athletics Championships each April. Coverage in Australia is mostly on Kayo Sports / ESPN, with free scores and results always available from World Athletics.

Swimming

Commonwealth Games complete · Dolphins won 76 medals
GAMES COMPLETE ▼
🏆 Commonwealth Games Swimming — a huge campaign
The Australian Dolphins topped the swimming medal table with 37 gold and 76 medals overall — a Games record. Meg Harris was the standout, winning six individual golds without a single loss.

Outside of the Commonwealth Games, the Australian Dolphins compete domestically at the Australian Swimming Championships (usually April/May, doubling as major-event trials) and internationally throughout the year.
