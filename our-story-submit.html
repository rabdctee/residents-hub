Margaret T. — Village resident since 1999
Early memories of the village
What did the village look like when you first moved in?

It was much smaller and quieter than it is today. There were far fewer villas and the gardens were still being established — you could see the open paddocks beyond the fence. It felt like a little outpost on the edge of the suburb.

What brought you here?

My husband and I had driven past the entrance on Ridley Road many times and always thought it looked lovely and well-kept. When the time came, we didn't have to think twice. We've never regretted it for a moment.
