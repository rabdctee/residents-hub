🎵 Lord Mayor's City Hall Concerts

September 2026 Programme — Brisbane City Hall
📍 Venue: Brisbane City Hall, King George Square, Brisbane City
🕛 Time: Every Tuesday, 12:00pm – 1:00pm
🎟️ Entry: Free admission — no bookings required
These concerts are presented by the Lord Mayor's City Hall Concert Series.
1
Sep
Liberace: The Man and the Music
Tuesday 1 September  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

A Dazzling One-Man Tribute to a Legendary Showman

Pianist Adam Przewlocki brings the glamour and showmanship of Liberace to life in this one-man show, mixing storytelling with skilled piano playing to retrace key moments from the entertainer's career.

Expect favourites from Tchaikovsky, Liszt and Gershwin alongside lighter crowd-pleasers, all delivered with the sparkle and charisma Liberace was famous for — candelabra included.

Brisbane Festival: David Bailey Silent Movie
Tuesday 8 September  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

A Classic Silent Comedy, Scored Live on the City Hall Organ

Part of this year's Brisbane Festival programme, organist David Bailey performs his own live score to Buster Keaton's classic railroad comedy The General, marking 100 years since the film was made.

It's a rare chance to hear City Hall's historic Willis pipe organ paired with a beloved piece of silent-film comedy.

Brisbane Festival: East of West
Tuesday 15 September  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

Balkan and Mediterranean Sounds Meet Contemporary Jazz

This ARIA-nominated trio — Bosnian-born composer Goran Gajić alongside Philip Griffin and Malindi Morris — blends Balkan and Mediterranean musical traditions with modern European jazz, another highlight of this year's Brisbane Festival.

Their music moves fluidly between styles, built on inventive composition and plenty of on-stage improvisation.

Brisbane Festival: UQ Music Gala
Tuesday 22 September  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

Brisbane's Rising Musical Talent Takes the Stage

The University of Queensland's finest emerging musicians present a lively gala hour of solo and ensemble performances, part of this year's Brisbane Festival programme.

Expect a varied mix of opera, choral pieces and more — a great chance to hear some of Brisbane's future musical stars up close.

Andrews Sisters Tribute
Tuesday 29 September  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

A Swinging Nostalgic Trip Through the 1940s–60s

Three harmony singers and a live swing band celebrate the golden era of close-harmony music, performing much-loved hits from the 1940s through to the 1960s.

Expect singalong favourites such as Boogie Woogie Bugle Boy, Rum and Coca-Cola and Candyman, plus a few surprises along the way.
