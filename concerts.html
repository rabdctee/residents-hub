🎵 Lord Mayor's City Hall Concerts

November 2026 Programme — Brisbane City Hall
📍 Venue: Brisbane City Hall, King George Square, Brisbane City
🕛 Time: Every Tuesday, 12:00pm – 1:00pm
🎟️ Entry: Free admission — no bookings required
These concerts are presented by the Lord Mayor's City Hall Concert Series.
3
Nov
Cinema & Standards: A Multi-Instrumental Tapestry
Tuesday 3 November  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

Where the Silver Screen Meets the Jazz Age

Brisbane-based multi-instrumentalist Haitch brings a genre-blending concert to City Hall, performing iconic movie scores from The Mission, Braveheart and Kill Bill on a range of exotic flutes.

The cinematic sound then gives way to American Songbook and light jazz favourites such as Summertime and Fly Me To The Moon, with heartfelt vocals and trumpet.

Nov
The Beatlegs
Tuesday 10 November  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

A Faithful Tribute to the World's Most Iconic Group

The Beatlegs are a Beatles tribute band dedicated to recreating the sound, style and spirit of the Fab Four, from the early days of Beatlemania through to the vibrant studio years.

Expect the harmonies and musicianship that defined a generation, performed with care and plenty of energy.

17
Nov
What a Wonderful World: The Music of Louis Armstrong
Tuesday 17 November  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

A Swinging Celebration of Satchmo's Songbook

Mal Wood and his swinging band explore the joy of Louis Armstrong's music, with favourites including What a Wonderful World, Hello Dolly!, Mack the Knife and When the Saints Go Marching In.

A captivating hour of fresh, live jazz that will have you singing along.

More info & details ↗
24
Nov
Tribute to the Heart of Scotland
Tuesday 24 November  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

Bagpipes, Fiddle and Highland Spirit

Step into the spirit of Scotland with soaring bagpipes, electrifying fiddle and favourites such as Caledonia, Flower of Scotland, Wild Mountain Thyme and Auld Lang Syne.

Featuring the acclaimed Australian Celtic Women and special guests, with ceilidh dancing, reels and Highland flings woven throughout.
