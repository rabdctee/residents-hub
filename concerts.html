🎵 Lord Mayor's City Hall Concerts

October 2026 Programme — Brisbane City Hall
📍 Venue: Brisbane City Hall, King George Square, Brisbane City
🕛 Time: Every Tuesday, 12:00pm – 1:00pm
🎟️ Entry: Free admission — no bookings required
These concerts are presented by the Lord Mayor's City Hall Concert Series.
13
Oct
Jodie-Joy Salutes Dusty Springfield and Divas
Tuesday 13 October  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

A Swinging '60s Tribute to the Great Divas

Powerhouse vocalist Jodie-Joy leads a live band show celebrating the golden age of 1960s music, drawing on a career that spans television, touring, cabaret and musical theatre.

Expect Dusty Springfield favourites like Son of a Preacher Man and Spooky, alongside other diva classics such as It's My Party — plenty to sing and dance along to.

20
Oct
Going For Gold with Christopher Wrench and Blake Humphreys
Tuesday 20 October  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

Trumpet and Organ Together on the City Hall Stage

Master organist Christopher Wrench joins trumpeter Blake Humphreys for a concert pairing City Hall's grand Father Henry Willis organ with the bright, golden tones of the trumpet.

The programme features works by Purcell, Haydn and Widor, among others.

27
Oct
Queensland Police Pipes and Drums & Band of Blue
Tuesday 27 October  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

Bagpipes, Drums and Brass from the Queensland Police

The Queensland Police Pipes & Drums, formed in 1958, are joined on stage by the Queensland Police Band of Blue for an afternoon spanning pipe band tradition alongside rock, pop, jazz and swing.

Both groups regularly perform at official Police and community events across the state, bringing a proud musical tradition to City Hall.
