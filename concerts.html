August 2026 Programme — Brisbane City Hall

Dance & Romance
Tuesday 4 August  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

A Magical Display of Dance and Organ Combined

Enjoy the spectacular display of Dance and Organ combined to produce a magical performance delighting in choreography displaying beauty and romance.

Benjamin Sheen, Director of Music at Jesus College, Cambridge, is an established concert artist on both sides of the Atlantic. Hailed as a "brilliant organist" by the New York Times, he is the 2013 winner of the Pierre S. du Pont First Prize in the inaugural Longwood Gardens Organ Competition. Benjamin holds degrees from the University of Oxford and the Juilliard School, and is a prize-winning Fellow of the Royal College of Organists.

Miss Mandy Swings ✦ Auslan Interpreted
Tuesday 11 August  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

Back to the Golden Days of Hollywood

Miss Mandy Swings takes you back to the golden days of Hollywood and the legendary ladies of stage and screen. Miss Mandy and her band bring to life Judy Garland, Liza Minnelli, Doris Day and Bette Midler, to name a few — backed by some of Brisbane's finest musicians: Ashley Higgins on drums, Sam Hewerdine on piano, Nick Woodend on bass and Darren McPherson on saxophone.

Expect onstage costume changes, cheeky banter and a delightful show with Miss Mandy swinging and dancing up a storm. Memories flood back with timeless songs like Over The Rainbow, Mack The Knife, At Last, New York New York and many more.

This performance is Auslan interpreted.

The Serenading Sopranos
Tuesday 18 August  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

Elegance, Warmth and Unforgettable Harmonies

The Serenading Sopranos are a vibrant ensemble of talented women whose harmonies have captivated audiences across South East Queensland. Gracing the stage in recent major musical productions — including Jesus Christ Superstar, The Sound of Music, Mary Poppins and My Fair Lady — they bring both experience and passion.

Beyond theatre, the Serenading Sopranos have delighted audiences at community carols, concerts at Brisbane City Hall, functions at Government House and as working solo performers across Brisbane and surrounding regions. Their diverse repertoire spans jazz, opera, classical, musical theatre, pop and folk, showcasing their versatility and rich vocal blend.

Produced by Viva La Musica.

The Acoustic Guitar Spectacular
Tuesday 25 August  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

Three Outstanding Australian Guitarists — One Exceptional Concert

The Acoustic Guitar Spectacular returns in 2026, bringing three outstanding Australian guitarists together for a concert of exceptional acoustic music. Hosted by internationally recognised guitarist Michael Fix, the concert highlights the skill, precision and creativity that have made this series a favourite with Queensland audiences.

Joining him are Jason McGregor — a national fingerstyle champion known for his versatile musicianship and soulful vocal work — and Craig Claxton, who brings over 50 years of experience shaped by blues, rock and jazz influences. Together they present a diverse programme spanning multiple styles, offering audiences a rare opportunity to experience three accomplished players sharing the stage.

Produced by Anje West.
