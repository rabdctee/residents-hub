🎵 Lord Mayor's City Hall Concerts

July 2026 Programme — Brisbane City Hall
📍 Venue: Brisbane City Hall, King George Square, Brisbane City
🕛 Time: Every Tuesday, 12:00pm – 1:00pm
🎟️ Entry: Free admission — no bookings required
These concerts are presented by the Lord Mayor's City Hall Concert Series.
7
Jul
Christopher Wrench — Organ Recital
Tuesday 7 July  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

Brisbane's Acclaimed Organist at the Iconic Father Henry Willis Organ

Internationally acclaimed Brisbane organist Christopher Wrench plays the iconic Father Henry Willis Organ at Brisbane City Hall. One of Australia's most respected organists, Christopher has performed at numerous festivals and on international tours around the world.

Christopher is currently Director of Music at Christ Church St Lucia, Chapel Music Director at St John's College (University of Queensland), and a member of the Brisbane City Council Willis Organ Advisory Committee.

The Best of Josh Groban featuring The Two Tenors
Tuesday 14 July  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

Soaring Harmonies Celebrating the Music of Josh Groban

Following the success of Groban's recent sell-out Australian tour, this elegant concert celebrates the songs that have captivated audiences worldwide. Former The Ten Tenors performer Roger Davy joins acclaimed musical theatre artist Mathew Leigh, accompanied by pianist Matt Rofe.

Together they perform favourites including You Raise Me Up, To Where You Are, Anthem and Bridge Over Troubled Water. Blending classical power with contemporary crossover style, this uplifting concert delivers rich harmonies, expressive storytelling and unforgettable vocal brilliance.

Hopelessly Devoted to Olivia
Tuesday 21 July  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

A Dazzling Celebration of the Timeless Music of Olivia Newton-John

Sharon Lee takes you on a dazzling journey celebrating the timeless music of Olivia Newton-John, backed by The Fabulous Four. This high-energy live production relives Olivia's biggest hits including Banks Of The Ohio, Let Me Be There, You're The One That I Want, Physical and Xanadu, with stunning vocals, dazzling costumes, sweet harmonies and captivating stage presence.

From the iconic songs of Grease to her beloved classics, the show captures the true essence, charm and spirit of Olivia's career. A nostalgia-filled adventure with feel-good moments, sing-along favourites and unforgettable entertainment.

The Spirit of the Isles — Queensland Conservatorium Brass Band
Tuesday 28 July  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

Celtic Fire and British Majesty — Conducted by Howard Taylor

Under the accomplished baton of conductor Howard Taylor, the Queensland Conservatorium of Music's brass band returns for another blockbuster concert. Always a highlight of the year, the band is accompanied by some very special guests.

Presenting a programme full of Celtic fire and British majesty, this is a concert not to be missed.
