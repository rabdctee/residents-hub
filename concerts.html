June 2026 Programme — Brisbane City Hall
📍 Venue: Brisbane City Hall, King George Square, Brisbane City
🕛 Time: Every Tuesday, 12:00pm – 1:00pm
🎟️ Entry: Free admission — no bookings required
These concerts are presented by the Lord Mayor's City Hall Concert Series.

Angela Fabian: The Magic of Whitney Houston
Tuesday 2 June  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

An Unforgettable Tribute to Whitney Houston

Join Angela Fabian and her powerhouse 8-piece band for an unforgettable tribute to Whitney Houston! Experience the magic of Whitney's greatest hits from the 80s and 90s, including classics like Saving All My Love for You, I Wanna Dance with Somebody, and So Emotional.

Get ready to be moved as Angela delivers a heartfelt homage in this spectacular celebration of one of the greatest voices of all time. Produced by Anje West.

A Tribute to the Great Ladies of Jazz — Simone Waddell
Tuesday 9 June  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

Ella, Billie, Nina, Sarah and More

Simone Waddell brings the celebrated songs of jazz's greatest female vocalists vividly to life. Widely regarded as one of Australia's leading contemporary jazz artists, Simone is praised for her "world-class voice," magnetic stage presence, and expressive storytelling.

This elegant concert honours legendary voices including Ella Fitzgerald, Billie Holiday, Nina Simone, Sarah Vaughan, Dinah Washington, Etta James, Norah Jones and Diana Krall — through a beautifully curated repertoire of timeless classics. From intimate ballads to uplifting swing, an experience audiences describe as spellbinding and utterly captivating.

Paradiso Strings: British Legends
Tuesday 16 June  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

60 Years of British Rock & Pop on Classical Instruments

A journey through the fantastic landscape of British music of the last 60 years. Paradiso Strings celebrate iconic tracks by The Beatles, Pink Floyd, Queen, Phil Collins, Radiohead and Arctic Monkeys, right through to current artists such as Ed Sheeran and Adele.

Paradiso Strings perform on classical instruments, bringing you the music as you've never heard it before, while fully capturing the power and emotion of these much-loved works.

Golden — Luke Kennedy, Irena Lysiuk & Damian Sim
Tuesday 23 June  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

A Dazzling Celebration of Award-Winning Golden Hits

All that glitters is gold in Golden — a dazzling musical mixtape from the award-winning creators of Christmas Actually and There's Something About Music. Superstar vocalists Luke Kennedy (The Voice Australia) and Irena Lysiuk join piano virtuoso Damian Sim for a sparkling celebration of golden hits.

Relive unforgettable soundtracks and iconic moments from stage and screen, including My Fair Lady, West Side Story, A Star Is Born and more. Featuring soaring vocals, brilliant musicianship and sparkling arrangements, Golden will dazzle and delight.

Danny Widdicomb and the Wandrin' Stars
Tuesday 30 June  |  12:00pm – 1:00pm

One Note at a Time — Old-Time Country with Heart and Grit

Danny Widdicombe and The Wandrin' Stars unveil One Note at a Time, a rich collection of old-time country songs delivered with heart, grit and understated virtuosity — the follow-up to their single Blue Eyes Cryin' in the Rain, paying homage to the great Willie Nelson.

A multi award-winning singer-songwriter, Danny brings together an all-star ensemble featuring Michel Rose on pedal steel, Luke 'Fiddleboy' Moller on fiddle, Brendan St Ledger on piano, Bos Borley on bass and Doug Gallacher. Danny's career spans stages alongside Bernard Fanning, Tim Rogers and Shane Nicholson, and includes work on Baz Luhrmann's film Elvis.
